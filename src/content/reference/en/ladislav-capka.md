**Long-term cooperation**

Over the past few years, Vojta Mareš and I have successfully tackled many technical challenges, primarily in the world of Kubernetes, where Vojta excels from both an operations and development perspective. Together we automated processes with scripts such as GitHub Workflows and GitLab pipelines, and handled complex infrastructures for public clouds (AWS, GCP) as well as our own RKE2 clusters with high-availability coverage. Vojta's expertise extends into development too -- his cross-disciplinary skills enabled us to build the foundational concept of a Kubernetes operator in Go, which is a key component of our product. Thanks to his experience and ability to quickly learn new things, the world of Kubernetes is an open book to him, and very little can catch him off guard.
