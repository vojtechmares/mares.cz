Looking for training but haven't found what you need among my existing courses? Let's arrange a custom training tailored to your needs and requirements.

## How it works

1. We have a call to specify what the training should cover and what it should not
2. We pick a suitable date
3. The training takes place, online or at your office
4. After the training, I send you all materials, which are yours to keep forever

## How much does it cost

The price depends on the scope of the training. Keep in mind that preparation is included in the price. Typically, it involves 1 man-day of training and 2+ man-days of preparation.
