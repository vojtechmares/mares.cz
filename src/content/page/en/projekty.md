## Terraform Cloudflare No Mail Module

A [Terraform](/en/blog/terraform-infrastruktura-jako-kod) DNS setup for blocking email sending so that a domain is not flagged as a spam source. And if you decide to send emails from the domain in the future, your domain reputation remains intact and you start with a clean slate.

[GitHub](https://github.com/vojtechmares/terraform-cloudflare-no-mail)
