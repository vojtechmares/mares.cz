## What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device when you visit a website. They help ensure the website functions properly, improve your user experience, and allow me to analyze traffic.

## What cookies does this website use?

This website only uses cookies for traffic measurement through Google Analytics. These cookies help me understand how you use the website, which allows me to improve it.

### Third-party cookies (Google Analytics)

Google Analytics uses cookies to anonymously collect information about website traffic. The data collected includes, for example:

- Number of website visitors
- Visit duration
- Pages visited
- Device type and browser

All data is anonymous and used solely for analytical purposes.

## How can you manage cookies?

You can manage or completely disable cookies through your browser settings. If you choose to reject cookies, some parts of the website may not function properly.

More information about managing cookies can be found at the following links:

- [Google Chrome](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647)
- [Mozilla Firefox](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/enable-and-disable-cookies-website-preferences)
- [Microsoft Edge](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/help/4027947/microsoft-edge-delete-cookies)
- [Safari](https://support.apple.com/guide/safari/sfri11471/mac)

## Changes to the cookie policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. I recommend checking this page periodically.
