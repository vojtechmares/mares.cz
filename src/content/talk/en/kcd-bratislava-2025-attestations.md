At KCD, after a year (this time in Bratislava), I gave a talk on attestations and OCI image signatures -- how to use them and how to validate image signatures and whitelist registries from which images in Kubernetes are allowed to run. Slides (PowerPoint presentation) are available for download [here](https://github.com/vojtechmares-examples/kcd-bratislava-2025-oci-attestations/raw/refs/heads/main/docs/slides.pptx).

If you want to try it yourself, check out the [demo project on GitHub](https://github.com/vojtechmares-examples/kcd-bratislava-2025-oci-attestations), the [recording on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7mFHxt7nlE4), or get started on your own with [Sigstore](https://www.sigstore.dev/) and [Cosign](https://github.com/sigstore/cosign)!
