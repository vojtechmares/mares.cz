Cloud promises elasticicy and cost savings, but the reality of most enterprise organizations is different: overflowing budgets, unreadable dashboards and developers who copy-paste resource configurations from StackOverflow or ChatGPT. FinOps is not just about cutting costs, it's about culture of shared responsibility of your cloud spendings. In this talk, I've shown a practical guide, how to embed FinOps principles into your organization on top of a Kubernetes platform. From resource configuration, Cluster Autoscaler and Karpenter configuration, and chareback model. No theory, no vendor demo. Just architecture, which works in production.
