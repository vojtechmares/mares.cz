I spoke about ways to secure your clusters, trust applications, monitor for data leaks, and defend against supply chain attacks.

I covered topics ranging from basic Kubernetes primitives like _securityContext_ and _NetworkPolicy_, through image signature verification, all the way to runtime-enforcement engines such as Falco, Tetragon, and KubeArmor.
